# Map attribution

Made with Natural Earth. Country geometry is public domain.

- Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson
- Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

`world-map.json` contains simplified 1:110m country paths and approximate representative country locations. Antarctica is omitted. Some small countries and territories are absent at this scale; their entries remain available in the dashboard list without a map marker.

Coordinates use Natural Earth label points, with interior-point corrections for Equatorial Guinea, New Zealand, and Trinidad and Tobago. Markers show country-level scope rather than exact factory or incident coordinates.
