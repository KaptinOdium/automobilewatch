From a list to a map

1. Sign in with your invited account. You and your team see the same saved supplier list.

2. Import CSV/TSV, or paste Excel cells with a header row. Name, DUNS, Country and City are required columns.

3. City + country names are first matched privately against 34,214 GeoNames cities. Supply latitude/longitude for precise placement, or allow online city lookups for unmatched rows. Only those place names are sent to Nominatim when you start a lookup.

4. Review ambiguous or missing locations. Multiple suppliers can share one city dot; select a cluster to see its members.

5. Click Save shared list to update the private shared workspace. Imports, coordinate edits and clearing stay unsaved until then. If someone else saves first, reload their version before saving again. Export CSV to download the filtered rows.

Accuracy: city-centre dots are approximate. DUNS values are preserved, not validated against D&B. A factory address is needed for site-level verification.

Privacy: supplier data is retrieved only after sign-in and membership checks. Signing out clears the working list from this tab. Downloaded CSV files remain on your computer.

Offline city data: GeoNames, CC BY 4.0. cities15000 downloaded 12 November 2026. The gazetteer is not a complete list of smaller towns.

Lookup service: small one-off batches only. Nominatim usage policy · Privacy policy.
